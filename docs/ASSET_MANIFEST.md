# Imported assets — V2

The build fetches 33 individually curated source files (about 5.3 MB), verifies SHA-256, imports actual meshes and exports self-contained GLBs. No model archive or vendor binary is committed. `tools/assets.lock.json` is the authoritative per-file URL, revision, byte count and checksum inventory; `tools/fetch_assets.py` rejects changed downloads.

Actual author LICENSE.txt files were inspected and are preserved in [licenses](licenses/). They explicitly allow personal, educational and commercial use. Runtime `LICENSES.html` credits the author. Buildings: castle_blue, barracks, home_A, home_B, blacksmith, market, tavern, archeryrange, tower_A, tower_B. Nature: tree_single_A/B and rock_single_B/C. GLB nodes retain `asset_source` metadata. The validator requires imported architecture AND nature, real skinning and both animations. One adapted 512px atlas serves world meshes; original UVs retained. Bespoke terrain, channel, bridge, tube cores, ferry, forecourts and department accents are generated by Telanico.

Kenney Fantasy Town Kit (official CC0 page) and Quaternius Medieval Village (official free CC0 listing) were evaluated. The selected KayKit author repositories offered reproducible direct downloads without a paid checkout or login. Kenney's city-builder starter was inspected but its modern road language was unsuitable. No paid asset or scraped marketplace archive is used.

The courier is a **temporary humanoid stand-in**, not the final Cooper/badger likeness. It is a genuine animated rig, not a primitive mascot. Replacing it later is a separate art task.
